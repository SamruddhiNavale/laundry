Loop Laundry Privacy Policy

Loop Laundry helps residents share washers and dryers. This policy applies to the Loop Laundry website and its mobile apps.

Information Loop uses

  • Room names, machine counts, cycle times, and queue positions to show the shared laundry status to room members.
  • A randomly generated device identifier and room access tokens to remember joined rooms and recognize a device’s loads and queue spots.
  • A push subscription or device token, when notifications are enabled, to deliver pickup reminders and queue-turn alerts through Apple, Google, or a browser push provider.

Loop does not require a name or email address to join. Avoid putting personal information in a room name. Anyone with a room code or invite link can join the room.

Storage and sharing

Loop uses its hosting and database providers to store room and queue data. Mobile apps keep saved access in the phone’s secure storage. The web app uses browser storage and cookies. Loop sends the device token and alert content to your notification provider to deliver a reminder.

Your choices

You can turn alerts off in Loop or your device settings. Leaving a room removes your saved membership for that device, removes your queue spots there, and stops its future reminders. Other residents can keep using the shared room. Clearing local app or browser data removes the saved access on your device.

For questions or help removing stored information, contact the Loop Laundry maintainer through the project’s support page. Please keep room codes, access tokens, and personal information out of public issues.
